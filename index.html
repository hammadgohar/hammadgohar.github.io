About

Ground engineering, backed by data and numerical rigor.

As Executive Manager and Lead Geotechnical Engineer at Asian Geomats (Pvt.) Ltd, I lead the full geotechnical lifecycle — from site investigation and geophysics through foundation and shoring design to instrumentation and third-party validation.

My work is grounded in a proprietary cross-validation soil database built from one of the region's largest investigation records, and sharpened by advanced numerical modeling and AI/ML-assisted analysis — delivering designs that are safer, buildable and value-engineered.
